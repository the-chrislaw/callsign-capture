Callsign Capture privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026.

Callsign Capture is an amateur-radio logging aid for iPhone. It listens through the microphone, turns what it hears into text on the phone, picks out callsigns and looks them up in a copy of the FCC licence database stored on the phone.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send audio, transcripts, callsigns, location or device identifiers anywhere.

Microphone

Audio is processed in memory on the phone and discarded. Transcripts and the callsigns found in them are kept in the app's own database on the phone so you can read your log later. They never leave the phone unless you export them yourself.

Speech recognition

The app transcribes with a Whisper model that runs on the phone. If that model has not been downloaded, it falls back to Apple's on-device speech recogniser; that path is covered by Apple's own privacy terms and, on supported devices, also stays on the phone.

Network use

The app connects to the network only when you ask it to: to download the Whisper speech model, to download the FCC licence directory from the address shown in Settings, or to download a directory from an address you typed yourself. Nothing is uploaded.

Your data

Your log is yours. Export it as ADIF from Settings, or delete the app to remove everything.
