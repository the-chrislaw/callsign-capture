Callsign Capture

Callsign Capture is an amateur-radio logging aid for iPhone. Leave it next to the speaker on a repeater, a net or a POTA activation and it listens, transcribes each transmission on the phone, picks out the callsigns and looks them up in a copy of the FCC licence database stored on the phone. No account, no cloud.

Common questions

Nothing is being logged. Check that the microphone permission is granted in Settings and that the phone is close to the radio's speaker. The Listen tab has a "Play a demo recording" button that proves the decoder works without a radio.

A callsign shows without a name. The FCC licence directory has not been downloaded yet; Settings offers the download.

Getting the log out. Settings exports the log as ADIF, which every logging program imports.
